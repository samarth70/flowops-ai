# Brag Plan: FlowOps AI

## What is this app?
FlowOps AI is an autonomous, multi-agent CRM Deal Desk that qualifies enterprise inbound inquiries, scores BANT criteria, executes margin risk analysis and discount governance, runs self-reflective critique, and synchronizes opportunities in real time.

## The angle
Enterprise sales reps spend 65% of their time on manual deal desk overhead, slow discount approvals, and messy CRM logging. FlowOps AI replaces this with a fleet of coordinated AI agents that ingest raw inquiries, calculate ICP & BANT scores, enforce margin guardrails via Human-in-the-Loop interceptors, and dispatch verified updates directly to your CRM.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A bold, high-contrast dark-mode card slams in with the burning RevOps dilemma: "Manual deal qualification is burning your sales velocity." A pulse wave sweeps across the screen as text resolves into "Autonomous Multi-Agent Deal Desk."

## Key moments (the middle)
1. **Agent Cascade in Motion**: Inbound deal drops in ($85,000 enterprise quote, 22% discount). Watch 4 specialized agents trigger sequentially with live latency counters and token metrics.
2. **BANT Scoring Matrix**: Dynamic animated gauge counts up 0 → 95/100, locking into "TIER 1 ENTERPRISE" badge with instant breakdown (Budget 25/25, Authority 25/25, Need 25/25, Timeline 20/25).
3. **Margin Governance & HITL Interceptor**: A warning banner alerts: "Discount 22% exceeds 15% margin guardrail → Routed to Sales Director Approval" with one-click decision flow.
4. **Instant CRM Kanban Sync**: The deal card glides seamlessly into the CRM "Proposal/Quote" column with full audit history and pitch draft generated.

## Outro / punchline
"Zero manual deal desk delays. 100% margin governance."
FlowOps AI logo mark resolves with live deployment URL: `flowops-ai.pages.dev`.

## User flow worth showing
- **Beat 1 (Entry)**: High-value inbound enterprise inquiry submitted.
- **Beat 2 (Agent Execution)**: Triage Agent extracts parameters → Qualification Agent calculates BANT score (95/100) → Strategist Agent caps discount & triggers HITL guardrail → Reflexion Critic polishes the pitch.
- **Beat 3 (Result)**: Real-time CRM Kanban board updates with full audit logs and trace waterfall.
